Siege of the Weeping Rift

Ranked duels against rivals of the Covenant. Rivals are matched to your trophies.

Monster models: KayKit Skeletons by Kay Lousberg and Ultimate Monsters by Quaternius (both CC0).

The Grimoire

Bind eight cards to your deck. Tap a card to bind or release it.

 

The Weeping Rift

No two lanes here. Three crossings, and the ground itself takes sides.

Three Crossings

Two long stone bridges on the flanks and a narrow causeway through the centre. The causeway is the shortest road to a spire, and the deadliest.

Spike Galleries

Iron plates on every crossing. They glow red, then erupt for 130 damage to any ground unit standing on them. Flyers pass untouched. Learn the rhythm.

Thornbrakes

Bramble thickets. Ground units inside are hidden: enemies and spires cannot target them unless they come within arm's reach. Attacking reveals them.

Weeping Mire

Tar pools at the foot of each bridge halve the speed of ground units wading through.

Altar of the Pale Saint

The island in the rift. Hold it with units alone for a few seconds and your souls flow 40% faster until the rival takes it back.

Spires & Crowns

Fell a Bell Tower for a crown and to claim ground beyond it. Fell the Cathedral Keep for all three. Three minutes, then sudden death.

Souls

Your currency. Ten at most, one every 2.8 seconds, twice as fast in the final minute.

Commands

Drag a card onto the field, or tap a card and then the ground. Keys 1–4 pick a card, Esc releases it.
