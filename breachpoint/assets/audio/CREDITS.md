# Sound credits

Every sound in this folder is a CC0 (public domain) recording. None is synthesized. Clips were trimmed,
high-passed at 30 Hz, peak-normalized to -1 dB and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis.

Mirrors used (the container could only reach GitHub):
- **GodotFall** `assets/audio/` (github.com/bonesvt/GodotFall): cuts of CC0 OpenGameArt/Kenney recordings, with per-file sources in its `assets/audio/sfx/README.md`.
- **open-game-sfx-index** (github.com/Legerdo/open-game-sfx-index): CC0 OpenGameArt packs, with license audit.
- **jlagun/gierka** `game/assets/audio/pistol_shot.wav`.
- **PanderMusubi/sound-effects-library-weapons**: PPSh sample.
