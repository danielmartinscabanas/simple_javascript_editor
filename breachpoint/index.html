Briefing · Dustline

A sandstone wall splits the map. Three ways through, each with its own trade-off.

Long Doors

The wide left gate. Easy to push through, but a proximity mine sits in the doorway and deep sand slows troops on the far side.

Mid Doors

The short road through the centre. Narrow, mined at both ends, and it runs straight over the supply drop.

Tunnels

The walled right passage. Ground troops inside are concealed: enemies and towers cannot target them unless they get close. Shooting gives them away.

Mines

A blinking red light means armed. The first ground unit to step on one triggers 190 damage around it, friend or foe. It re-arms after 9 seconds.

Supply Drop

Hold the pad in Mid with troops alone for a few seconds and your funds flow 40% faster until the rival takes it back.

Smoke

Smoke grenades conceal everything inside the cloud for 7 seconds. Use them to walk a push past a tower.

Towers & Crowns

Destroy an Outpost for a crown and ground to deploy on. Destroy the Command Post for all three. Three minutes, then sudden death.

Funds

Ten at most, one every 2.8 seconds, twice as fast in the final minute. Drag a card onto the field, or tap a card then the ground. Keys 1–4 also work.
