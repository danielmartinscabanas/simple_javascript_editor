Breachpoint

Tactical Tower Assault

Three-lane ranked matches against rival squads, matched to your trophies.

Models: Quaternius & Kenney · Audio: Free Firearm Sound Library, OpenGameArt artists, Kenney · all CC0

Loadout

Pick eight cards for your deck. Tap a card to add or remove it.

 

Briefing · Dustline

Three lanes, a jungle of alleys between them and a dry riverbed across the middle. Eight minutes to break the rival Command Post.

Lanes & outposts

Long (left), Mid and Tunnels (right) each have an outer and an inner outpost. An inner outpost is shielded until the outer one in its lane falls; the Command Post is shielded until any inner outpost falls. Destroy the Command Post to win.

Recruit waves

Every 30 seconds both bases send a squad of recruits down every lane. Your cards march the nearest lane when they have nothing to shoot.

River objectives

Hold a pad with troops alone to capture it. Supply Drop: funds +40%. Armory: your troops deal +15% damage. Med Station: your troops heal 1.5% per second.

Veterancy

Troops earn XP while fighting, for damage and for kills, and rank up to level 4 (chevrons over their heads): more health, damage and fire rate. XP a card's troops earn also promotes the card, so later deployments arrive at a higher level.

Deploying

Anywhere on your side of the river, plus a forward zone around every rival outpost you destroy. Each outpost you take also pays $2.

Tunnels & mines

The walled stretch of Tunnels at the river hides troops inside. Proximity mines blink red when armed and hit everything on the ground nearby.

Camera

Drag the map to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap the minimap to jump. Keyboard: WASD or arrows, +/- to zoom, Space to return to base, 1–4 for cards.

Time

Eight minutes, funds twice as fast in the last two. If no Command Post falls, more outposts destroyed wins; level after two minutes of sudden death goes to the healthier side.
